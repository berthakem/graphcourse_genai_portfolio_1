About me

Making healthcare data clearer, more inclusive, and more useful.

I work at the intersection of health information systems, data governance, analytics, and digital transformation. Through Tangaza, I am exploring how generative AI can help turn complex health information into clearer insights, better workflows, and more accessible knowledge for decision-makers and communities.

About the course

Final project

From learning to a practical AI artefact.

The final project is still being shaped. The direction will remain aligned with Tangaza: using AI to bring clarity, structure, and insight to health data, public health knowledge, or research workflows.

Potential focus ✦ Tangaza

A practical AI workflow for health data insight and communication.

The final artefact may explore how generative AI can support evidence synthesis, data storytelling, governance documentation, dashboard interpretation, or decision-ready briefing products for public health teams.

✦
Idea 01
AI insight brief generator A guided workflow that turns health data notes into clear, non-technical briefing summaries.
Idea 02
Health data governance assistant A structured assistant for documenting datasets, data quality checks, and responsible use considerations.
Idea 03
Research synthesis companion A tool-assisted process for organizing literature, extracting themes, and producing accessible knowledge products.
